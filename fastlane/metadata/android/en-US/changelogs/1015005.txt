Fifth ZenPipe development release: hardening and practice themes.
• Seeking to the end no longer skips the mandatory break (at least half of the way must really be played)
• Practice playlists are frozen when activated; new videos count as practice after 24 h
• Videos under 5 minutes: low priority in the feed and a fixed 60 s break after each
• Feed: the "why" line is a short phrase instead of numbers (numbers stay in the review view)
• TalkBack: the break countdown is announced once a minute, +/- buttons are named
• "Done" in the first-time setup asks before it discards open drafts; "Never mind" now says "Close app"
• Practice themes per video (fitness, yoga, sleep, meditation, breathing) with chips in the Practice tab; default theme per channel/playlist and corrections in the review view
• Single videos can be marked as practice (24 h lock like channels)
• A calm question once a day when practice time gets long (threshold in the Zen settings)
• A restored NewPipe backup ends the free first-time setup (your rules start strict)
• Feed editions also follow the device uptime; the media browser (Android Auto) no longer lists history or Shorts
• Old export files in the cache are deleted after an hour
