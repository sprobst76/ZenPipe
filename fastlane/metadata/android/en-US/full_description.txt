ZenPipe is a calm, privacy-friendly YouTube client for Android: watch what you decided to watch, then stop. It is a fork of NewPipe with a different goal: mindful viewing instead of an endless feed.

<b>What is different from NewPipe</b>
• A feed with an end: a limited number of unseen videos from your subscriptions per edition, a daily cap, no infinite scrolling.
• No autoplay, no auto-queue, no related videos, no comments, no Shorts.
• An intention check before you start watching: a short pause to decide what you want to watch and when you are done.
• A mandatory break after you abort a video early, so skipping around does not become a habit.
• Zen settings that are protected by a 24-hour lock: loosening the rules takes a day, tightening them is immediate.
• Everything stays on your device: no account, no analytics, no crash reports sent automatically.

<b>What you keep from NewPipe</b>
• No Google services and no YouTube API: the app parses the website, so it works without Google Play Services.
• Subscriptions without an account, local playlists, background and popup playback, downloads.
• Free software under the GPLv3.

<b>Not affiliated</b>
ZenPipe is an independent fork of NewPipe (https://newpipe.net) and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TeamNewPipe. ZenPipe installs next to NewPipe and does not use NewPipe's update or crash-report servers.
